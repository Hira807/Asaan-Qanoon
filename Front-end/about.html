About

Legal knowledge should be for everyone.

Asaan Qanoon was built on a simple belief: no one should feel helpless in the face of a legal problem because they cannot afford a lawyer or understand legal language.

Our Mission

Democratizing access to legal information in Pakistan

Pakistan has over 220 million citizens, yet access to basic legal knowledge remains concentrated among the educated elite. Most citizens — facing wage theft, wrongful evictions, fraudulent contracts, or domestic abuse — have no idea what rights they hold or what laws protect them.

Asaan Qanoon bridges this gap using artificial intelligence. You describe your problem in plain Urdu or English, and the system identifies the applicable Pakistani law, explains it simply, and tells you what to do next.

Plain Language

Complex legal statutes are translated into clear, jargon-free explanations anyone can understand.

Bilingual

Full support for both Urdu and English — talking to you in the language of your choice.

Always Available

Get instant legal clarity whenever you need it, with zero wait times or appointments.

Free Access

No hidden costs or subscriptions — purely built to maximize digital public legal literacy.

Academic Project

This is an academic prototype

Asaan Qanoon is an academic research and design project, not a commercial product. It is developed to explore how AI can improve access to justice in Pakistan. The information provided is for educational purposes only. For legal matters, always consult a licensed lawyer registered with the Pakistan Bar Council.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this legal advice?

No. Asaan Qanoon provides educational insights to help you understand your legal landscape. It does not replace a physical consultation or formal representation by a qualified lawyer.

How accurate is the AI guidance?

Our algorithms index cross-verified statutory laws, regulatory gazettes, and legal databases. While highly precise for structural definitions, users must check real-time changes through legal practitioners.

Is my data private?

Completely. Queries processed are completely anonymized and never shared with commercial brokers or third-party networks. We do not store tracking records tied directly to your offline identity.

Does it work in Urdu?

Yes. You can comfortably query the ecosystem using Romanized Urdu text, standard Nastaliq scripts, or English prose. The engine answers fluently in your selected language input option.

Is this free to use?

Yes, Asaan Qanoon is fully open and completely free. It operates as an open-access public resource aimed at expanding civic legal literacy across all communities in Pakistan.
